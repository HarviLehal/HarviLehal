# Hi there, I’m Harvi! 👋
I’m a PhD researcher in **computational statistics and Bayesian inference** at the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Distributed Algorithms, University of Liverpool, working in collaboration with the UK Government. I specialise in exact sampling methods for likelihoods that are irregular, noisy and expensive to compute, including subsampling MCMC, Sequential Monte Carlo and Bayesian decision trees.

- 🔭 I’m currently writing up my thesis, *Developing efficient numerical algorithms using fast Bayesian random trees*, for submission in December 2026.
- 💼 I’m looking for **postdoctoral research roles** in Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification and numerical methods, available from January 2027.
- 👀 I’m interested in Bayesian Statistics, MCMC & SMC, Data Classification, Psephology and Machine Learning.
- 🔬 My work: benchmarking subsampling MCMC against HINTS, and building directed proposals for Bayesian decision trees in MCMC and SMC.
